# Privacy Policy for Volume Master Max

**Effective Date:** July 27, 2026

## Overview

Volume Master Max ("we", "our", or "extension") is committed to respecting and protecting user privacy. This Privacy Policy explains our data practices for the Volume Master Max browser extension.

## Data Collection and Usage

**Volume Master Max DOES NOT collect, store, log, transmit, or share any personal or user data.**

- **No Analytics or Tracking**: We do not use third-party analytics, tracking scripts, telemetry, or remote code.
- **No Remote Servers**: Volume Master Max operates entirely offline on your local device.
- **Local Storage Only**: Preferences such as dark mode settings, equalizer presets, and volume scroll direction are saved locally on your browser via `chrome.storage.local`. This data never leaves your device.

## Browser Permissions

Volume Master Max requests the following permissions strictly to provide core audio functionality:

- `activeTab` & `tabs`: To identify browser tabs playing audio and present per-tab volume controls.
- `tabCapture` & `offscreen`: To capture and route audio through local Web Audio API nodes for volume amplification up to 600% and equalization.
- `storage`: To save your user preferences locally on your browser.

## Third-Party Services

Volume Master Max does not use any third-party services, APIs, or remote code execution.

## Policy Certification

Volume Master Max complies with the Chrome Web Store Developer Program Policies:
1. We do not sell or transfer user data to third parties.
2. We do not use user data for purposes unrelated to the extension's single purpose.
3. We do not use user data for creditworthiness or lending purposes.
